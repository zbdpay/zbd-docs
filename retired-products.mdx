ZBD Ramp has been retired, so its documentation is no longer on this site. If you followed an old link, it brought you here.

| Product | What it was |
| --- | --- |
| ZBD Ramp | A fiat-to-Bitcoin onramp, embedded with the Ramp SDKs |

If you have an existing integration with Ramp, reach out to your ZBD contact.

## What ZBD offers today

- [Embedded Payouts](/embedded-payouts): pay creators, sellers, and players from a pool you fund
- [Embedded Accounts](/embedded-accounts): give players a balance inside your game they can earn into, spend, send, and cash out
- [Embedded Rewards](/embedded-rewards): add real-money rewards to your Unity mobile game
- [Bitcoin](/bitcoin/api): ZBD's Bitcoin and Lightning payments API
