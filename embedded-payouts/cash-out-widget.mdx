It embeds in your product as an iframe, and you choose which of its flows to use.

## Flows

The widget is made up of separate flows you can use together or on their own. [Choosing your integration](/embedded-payouts#choosing-your-integration) covers what each one does.

## How it fits

Your backend creates the [user](/embedded-payouts/users), credits their balance if ZBD holds it, and reads status. The widget covers everything the user sees and everything collected from them.

## Integration steps

1. Create the user
2. Credit their balance, if ZBD holds it
3. Create a session
4. Embed the widget in your frontend
5. Listen for events

The first three steps run on your server, so your API key never reaches the browser. See [Create a Session](/embedded-payouts/apis/create-session) for the session call.
