The cash out widget is a hosted flow that runs the whole player-facing side of taking money out: identity verification, tax information, linking a [payout account](/embedded-payouts/methods-and-coverage), and the cash out itself. It embeds in your product as an iframe.
## When to use it
Use the widget when you want a payout program live without building the player-facing experience yourself.
Use components instead when you have an existing flow you want to keep, and place the individual pieces inside it.
Both collect and store the same data on ZBD's side, so the choice is about how much of the experience you compose.
## How it fits
Your backend does what it always does: create the [user](/embedded-payouts/users), credit their balance, read status. The widget covers what the player sees and everything collected from them.
## Integration shape
1. Create the user
2. Credit their balance
3. Create a session
4. Embed the widget in your frontend
5. Listen for events
The first three run on your server, so your API key never reaches the browser.
Full detail in Prerequisites, Integration flow and Embedding the widget.
