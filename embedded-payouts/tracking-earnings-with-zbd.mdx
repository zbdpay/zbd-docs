Use this model if you'd rather not build and reconcile your own record of what users are owed. You tell ZBD each time a user earns, and ZBD keeps the running total. Your pool only needs to cover what's cashed out, when it's cashed out.

## How it works

1. [Create the user](/embedded-payouts/users) from your backend.
2. Credit their earnings each time they earn.
3. Show their earnings and history in your product.
4. The user cashes out through the widget, verifying and choosing a payment method as part of the flow if they haven't already.
5. Track the result by webhook.

## Verification

Because ZBD sees what each user earns and cashes out, verification can be tiered. Users can start earning and cash out small amounts at a lower tier, and verify further when a cash out needs it. The cash out flow asks for it at that point, so you don't have to track tiers yourself.

You can still run full verification up front, for example at signup, if you'd rather users are cleared before they earn anything.

## Crediting earnings

Each [credit](/embedded-payouts/apis/fund-user) adds to what the user is owed. Nothing is drawn from your pool when you credit, only when the user cashes out.

Send an idempotency key with every credit, so a retry doesn't credit the user twice.

## Reversing a credit

To take back a credit, for example when a reward is canceled, [debit the user](/embedded-payouts/apis/deplete-user). A debit only succeeds while the user still has those earnings available, so it can't recover an amount they've already cashed out.

## Showing earnings

Use [Get Earnings](/embedded-payouts/apis/get-balance) and [List User Transactions](/embedded-payouts/apis/list-user-transactions) to show users what they've earned and what they've been paid.

## Cashing out

The user cashes out through the widget's cash out flow. At that moment, the amount comes out of your pool and off the user's earnings. See [Embedding the Widget](/embedded-payouts/cash-out-widget).

## When a cash out fails or is returned

The amount goes back to the user's earnings, so they can cash out again once the problem is fixed. A webhook tells you either way.

## Holds

Some cash outs may be held before they're paid. A held cash out releases on its own, and it's worth showing users the release date instead of a generic pending status.
