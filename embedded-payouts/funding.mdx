The funds need to be in your pool when a payout is initiated. You can credit users, on your side or on ZBD's, before then.

## How funding works

1. You send funds from your treasury to the account ZBD provides for your organization.
2. The funds land in your pool, in your funding currency.
3. Once they settle, the full balance is available to pay out.

Funding is always a push from your side. ZBD never pulls from your account.

## Funding currency and conversion

Your pool holds one funding currency. Users are paid in [their own currency](/embedded-payouts/methods-and-coverage), converted at the rate quoted at the time of the payout, so the amount leaving your pool can differ from what the user receives.

## Keeping the pool funded

- **Track your balance.** Your ZBD contact can set up balance reporting for your own monitoring.
- **Agree a low-balance level.** Work with ZBD on the level that should prompt a top-up.
- **Fund ahead of volume.** If your volume is predictable, fund against your expected run rate instead of waiting for alerts.

## When the pool is short

If the pool can't cover a payout when it's initiated, the payout fails the [funds check](/embedded-payouts/paying-out) and nothing is paid. Top up and re-instruct. Users aren't shown a failure caused by funding, but payouts stop until you top up.

## Settlement timing

Funds become available once they settle with ZBD, and timing depends on how you send them. Plan top-ups so users aren't left waiting on your funding to settle.
