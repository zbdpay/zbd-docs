title: "Users"

A user is the person on the receiving end, someone who holds a balance with you and gets paid out. It's the same object across Embedded Payouts and Embedded Accounts, so a user you create for payouts is the same user if you later run an economy on top.
You create them from your backend, ZBD verifies them, and each one carries a verification tier and a payout account.
## When to create and verify
This is your call and it depends on your program. Both patterns are supported.

|   | Up front | Just in time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What happens | Create and verify users before anyone earns | Create when someone earns, verify when a payout requires it |
| Suits | A known set of users, contracted creators, programs where payout is expected | Large pools of casual earners where most never reach a payout |
| Trade-off | Friction for people who may never cash out | Friction lands at the moment someone wants their money |

The constraints hold either way. A user has to exist before you can [credit a balance](/embedded-payouts/how-payouts-work) to them, and verification has to clear before a [payout](/embedded-payouts/paying-out) above their current tier.
## Creating a user
Create a user with your own identifier for that person, and no personal data is needed at this point.
## Verification
Verification is tiered. A user's tier sets how much they can be paid, and they move up as cumulative earnings grow or whenever you prompt them to.
Where the verification step appears in your product is up to you. ZBD tells you when a payout needs a higher tier than the user currently holds.
It runs through ZBD either way, inside the [cash out widget](/embedded-payouts/cash-out-widget) or as a component you place in your own flow.
## Tax information
Tax information is collected when a payout requires it, or at onboarding if you'd rather get it out of the way early. Which form applies depends on where the user is and where you are, and ZBD works that out rather than asking you to.
It's collected once and reused, so a user who has already provided it isn't asked again.
## Payout account
The user links the account their money lands in, choosing from the [options available in their country](/embedded-payouts/methods-and-coverage). This happens in the widget, and the account details are collected and held by ZBD.
A user without a linked payout account can still hold a balance, they just can't be paid until they link one.
## Reading user state
You can read back a user's tier, whether they've linked a payout account, and whether they're payable right now. That's usually enough to decide what your product shows them, whether that's a cash out button, a prompt to verify, or a prompt to link an account.
