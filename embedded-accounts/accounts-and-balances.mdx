Every player in your economy has an account with ZBD. It holds their balances, records everything that moves, and carries the permissions that decide what they can do.
## The objects

| Object | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| User | The player. You create one using your own identifier for them. |
| Account | Their financial record with ZBD. One per user. |
| Wallet | Where a balance in a given currency lives. Wallets are currency-specific. |

Creating a user provisions the account and a wallet for each currency in your project, so nothing else is needed before you can credit them.
## Creating an account costs the player nothing
You create accounts from your backend with an identifier of your own, a country code, and confirmation the player accepted your terms. No identity documents and no personal data.
Identity checks happen later. A player can hold a balance, earn and spend without being asked for anything. Verification is required when the user initiates one of the following actions: loading a fiat wallet, selling an item for fiat, or cashing out.
## What a balance actually is
A balance isn't a number ZBD stores and updates. It's the sum of every entry in the ledger for that wallet, and each entry carries a source tag recording where the value came from.
So a player's balance has structure. The total is what they can spend, while what they can take out depends on which tags make up that total. A player showing 1,700 might be able to cash out all, some or none of it.
[Currencies](/embedded-accounts/currencies) covers how tags work and what each one permits. ZBD sets that, not you.
## More than one wallet
Each currency gets its own wallet automatically. You can also create additional wallets against the same currency where you want to keep value separate, for example keeping a seasonal event currency apart from a player's main balance.
## Your own wallet
Your organization holds a wallet per currency, and funds a separate project wallet — also one per currency — for each game. Keeping only a game's expected payouts in its project wallet limits what a leaked key or fraud can reach.
## Reading balances
You can read a wallet's balance, the entries behind it, and what portion is currently available to cash out. Read these rather than tracking balances yourself, since tags and conversions affect what a player can do with what they hold.
