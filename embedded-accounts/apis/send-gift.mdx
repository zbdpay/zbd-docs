<Info>
  **Requires activation.** Available once ZBD switches this feature on for your account. Until then it returns a 503 — that means the feature is off, not that the API is down. Ask your ZBD contact to enable it.
</Info>

Player → player gift, never a publisher key. Recipient by **exactly one** of `user_id` / `publisher_user_id`; both or neither is a 400. Gifter comes from the session, never the body. Service enforces **0.1–1000 whole units**, tighter than the DTO's `minimum: 1` — validate against the service rule

<ParamField header="Authorization" type="string" required>
  `Bearer` session + `Idempotency-Key`
</ParamField>
