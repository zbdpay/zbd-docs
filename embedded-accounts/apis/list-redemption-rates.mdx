---
title: "List redemption rates"
description: "Read your rates — take the row with is_current: true."
api: "GET https://api.zbdpay.com/api/v1/publishers/{publisherId}/redemption-rates"
---

<Info>
  **Requires activation.** Available once ZBD switches this feature on for your account. Until then it returns a 503 — that means the feature is off, not that the API is down. Ask your ZBD contact to enable it.
</Info>

Read your rates — take the row with `is_current: true`. Filters: `from_currency_code`, `to_currency_code`, `current_only`, `skip`, `take`. Rates are append-only and forward-only; there is no update or delete route, and writes are ZBD-side

<ParamField header="Authorization" type="string" required>
  `x-api-key`
</ParamField>
