---
title: "Cash outs"
description: "Moving value out of a player's balance and into an account they control."
---

Cashing out moves value out of a player's balance and into an account they control. Whatever your economy runs on, this is the point where value leaves the system.
## What has to be true first
Three things gate a cash out, and all of them are worth surfacing in your game well before a player reaches this point.
**The value has to be eligible.** A balance is made of pools with different histories, and not all of them can leave. That includes fiat: money a player put in is not money they can take back out this way.
**The player has to be verified.** No verification is needed to hold, earn or spend. It's required to take money out, and the level of verification affects how much can leave and how quickly. Selling in a marketplace requires it too, where the sale settles in fiat.
**A payout account has to be linked.** The player chooses where their money lands from the options available in their country.
## Conversion
What happens here depends on what the player holds.
A player holding fiat is already holding money, so nothing converts.
A player holding a currency you defined has it converted at your redemption rate, applied at the moment they cash out.
Where a conversion happens, show the player the resulting amount before they commit. Someone who expected one number and received another will assume they were charged a fee nobody mentioned.
## Where the money goes
Money settles to the account the player linked, on a rail available in their country. Fees for the method they chose come out of their proceeds, so what arrives is lower than what left their balance.
The player picks the method, and faster ones generally cost more, so they're trading speed against what lands.
## Funding it
Cash outs draw from your project wallet, so value leaving the system has to be covered by what you've funded.
We recommend funding against the volume you expect to exit over a full settlement period, with headroom for a busier week than usual.
## What to show players
Most support load around cash out is about waiting and limits, and nearly all of it is preventable in your own interface.
A player should be able to see what's available now, what isn't yet, what would change that, and where a submitted cash out has got to. A player who can answer those questions without asking doesn't contact anyone.
