Cashing out is where value leaves your economy.

## Before a player can cash out

Three things need to be in place. It's worth showing players where they stand on each well before they try to cash out.

- **Eligible value.** Not every part of a balance can be cashed out. This includes fiat: money a player loaded can't be withdrawn this way.
- **Verification.** Players don't need to verify to hold, earn or spend, but they do to cash out. Their verification level affects how much they can take out and how quickly. Selling in a marketplace for fiat also requires it.
- **A payment method.** The player chooses how to get paid from the options available in their country.

## Conversion

- **Fiat:** nothing converts.
- **Your own currency:** converted at your redemption rate at the moment of cash out.

Show the player the final amount before they confirm. If they receive a different number than they expected, they'll assume there was a hidden fee.

## Where the money goes

Money settles to the payment method the player chose, on a rail available in their country. Fees for that method come out of the amount, so what arrives is less than what left their balance. Faster methods generally cost more.

## Funding cash outs

Cash outs draw from your project wallet, so it needs to cover what players take out. We recommend funding against the volume you expect over a full settlement period, with room for a busier week than usual.

## What to show players

Most cash out support tickets are about waiting and limits, and you can head off most of them in your own interface. Show players:

- What they can cash out now
- What they can't cash out yet, and what would change that
- Where a submitted cash out is up to
