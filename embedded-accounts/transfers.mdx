## How a transfer starts

The sending player authorizes every transfer, so it runs on their session and starts with something they did in your game. Your backend can't move value between two players on its own, because a transfer gives away something the player owns.

## What the receiving player gets

Value received by transfer gets a new source tag. That tag decides what the receiving player can do with it, including whether they can cash it out later. [Currencies](/embedded-accounts/currencies) covers source tags.

## Why transfers are useful

Transfers let value move between players, as well as between you and your players. That's what makes guilds, favors, trades outside the marketplace and gifts between friends possible, and it makes your currency feel like something players own.

## Limits and checks

ZBD sets and runs limits and checks on transfers. Transfers are a common route for moving value for reasons that have nothing to do with play, so they get more scrutiny than spends.

These controls change over time. Build against the response you get instead of assuming fixed rules, and show a refused transfer to the player as a normal outcome instead of an error.
